Top level creative parameters such as title, link_url, url_tags, body, and many others (full list here ) can now be overwritten (edited) for the copied ad, whereas previously, all fields were copied over into the new ad and were unable to be changed. This is not a breaking change, therefore no immediate action is required on your end. This is an ad hoc release to provide developers with more flexibility when copying their ads.
