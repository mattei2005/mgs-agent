# Add partners to your business portfolio

You can add partners (example: an agency you work with) to your organization’s business portfolio in Meta Business Suite by either:

*   [Giving the partner access to your organization’s business assets](https://facebook.com/business/help/1717412048538897) (example: Facebook Page) 
*   [Asking a partner to share assets with your organization](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/408759743051505)
