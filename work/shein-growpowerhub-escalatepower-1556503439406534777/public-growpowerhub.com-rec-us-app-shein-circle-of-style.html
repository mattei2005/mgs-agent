SHEIN FREE TRIAL GUIDE

Imagine exploring a new clothing style and sharing what you discover with other shoppers. That is the idea behind SHEIN’s Free Trial Program.

Selected participants receive an item to try and review. The interesting part goes beyond opening the package: you get to explore its fit, feel, and styling possibilities.

Start With a Style You Want to Explore

Choose an available item you can picture wearing with your own clothes. A familiar outfit gives the trial a useful starting point.

An interesting neckline, a different silhouette, or another shade can inspire a fresh combination. Your first styling idea helps guide what you observe.

Turn the Trial Into a Useful Style Story

If selected, try the garment with the shoes and layers you normally wear. Notice how the complete outfit feels as you sit, walk, and move.

Capture the details another shopper would want to see. Material, measurements, and your own photographs make the story more informative.

  • Material: notice texture, thickness, and movement.
  • Construction: examine seams, closures, and finishing.
  • Fit: compare the garment with its size information.
  • Styling: describe how you used it in an outfit.
REVIEW NOTES

A useful review turns an outfit into a story other shoppers can picture. Describe fit, texture, movement, and the details you noticed while wearing it.

A Few Questions Before You Explore

Do I need to be an influencer to participate?

⌄

SHEIN USA lists active accounts belonging to adults aged 18 or older as eligible. You can explore the program without being an influencer.

Are all shipping costs covered?

⌄

SHEIN USA covers shipping for trial items. Customs duties can still apply, so review the delivery details for the opportunity in your account.

What if the product doesn’t fit?

⌄

Describe the fit clearly, including the selected size and where the garment sits. Specific observations can help another shopper choose.
