## Confirmed problem

The 30-day trusted-device cookie was valid and user-generic, but the ordinary `Sair` endpoint revoked that device and cleared its cookie. This made the next password login require MFA before 30 days. Browser/profile changes, cookie clearing, expiry, security revocation and a changed user-agent continue to require MFA by design.

Production baseline showed live trusted-device reuse for Rodolfo, Isliago, Kelly and Nicolas. Ícaro's prior device had been legitimately revoked in the authorized 20 September credential recovery; Joe and Geizian had no valid trusted-device registration at the time of diagnosis. The same-browser implementation test passed for all seven identities.

## Published behavior

- Ordinary `Sair` revokes only the 8-hour/3-hour-idle session and preserves an unexpired trusted-device cookie.
- The new `Sair e esquecer este dispositivo` action revokes and clears only that browser's trusted-device cookie.
- Password remains mandatory after every session expiry/logout.
- Trust remains user-bound, browser/user-agent-bound, capped at five active devices and limited to 30 days.
- New audit actions record successful trust reuse, rejected trust with a credential-free reason, and explicit device forgetting. `LOGOUT` records whether trust was preserved, forgotten or absent.
